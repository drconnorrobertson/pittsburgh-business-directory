# Editorial review workflow

This directory has legacy profiles that require verification. A submission email is a candidate, not a published listing.

## Intake to publication

1. Record the candidate business name, website, submitter contact, and date in the editorial queue. Keep private contact information out of public GitHub issues and commits.
2. Verify the business exists using its own website or an authoritative public record. Record source URLs and access dates in the PR description.
3. Verify the displayed address, phone number, site URL, founder identity, founding date, and any numerical or superlative claim. Remove a claim if it cannot be supported.
4. Check that images are licensed or owned and that quoted testimonials or interview material have clear provenance.
5. Create or update the profile in a pull request. Have another reviewer compare every factual claim with the recorded sources.
6. Regenerate search/profiles.json from approved public profile text. Update the sitemap when a URL is added or removed.
7. After publication, review correction requests promptly and preserve a record of substantive edits.
