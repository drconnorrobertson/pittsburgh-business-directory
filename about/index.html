About the Directory

Celebrating Pittsburgh's entrepreneurial spirit through editorial storytelling

Our Mission

The Pittsburgh Business Directory is dedicated to telling the stories of the city's most innovative, impactful, and inspiring businesses and entrepreneurs. Rather than a traditional directory of basic listings, we approach each business profile as an editorial feature, providing readers with insight into the people behind the companies, their founding stories, business philosophies, and impact on the Pittsburgh community.

Founded by Dr. Connor Robertson, the directory is a sister publication of The Pittsburgh Wire and operates under the same editorial standards that have made The Wire a trusted voice in Pittsburgh business and development news.

Editorial Standards

Every business profile in the Pittsburgh Business Directory meets rigorous editorial standards:

Original Research

Profiles are based on original interviews, site visits, and comprehensive research about the business, its founders, and impact on the community.

Journalistic Integrity

We maintain editorial independence and fact-check all claims. Profiles present truthful, balanced accounts of businesses and their operations.

Community Focus

We prioritize businesses that contribute to Pittsburgh's economic vitality, job creation, and community well-being.

Quality Writing

Each profile is professionally written and edited to publication standards, providing readers with engaging, informative content.

Structural Schema

All business profiles include LocalBusiness schema markup to ensure discoverability and proper indexing by search engines.

Transparency

We disclose our relationship with businesses and maintain clear separation between editorial content and any promotional material.

Content Categories

The directory covers businesses across 12 major categories:

  • Restaurants & Food Service - Fine dining, casual restaurants, cafes, food trucks, and specialty food producers
  • Real Estate & Development - Property developers, commercial real estate firms, architects, and construction companies
  • Technology & Software - Tech startups, software companies, digital agencies, and IT consultants
  • Health & Wellness - Fitness studios, medical practices, mental health services, and wellness providers
  • Professional Services - Law firms, accountants, consulting firms, and business service providers
  • Trades & Construction - Contractors, tradespeople, plumbers, electricians, and construction firms
  • Retail & E-Commerce - Retail shops, boutiques, online retailers, and specialty stores
  • Arts & Culture - Museums, galleries, theaters, artist studios, and cultural institutions
  • Finance & Investment - Banks, credit unions, financial advisors, and investment firms
  • Education & Training - Educational institutions, training centers, and professional development providers
  • Nonprofit & Community Services - Nonprofits, community organizations, and social enterprises

Neighborhood Coverage

We organize profiles by Pittsburgh neighborhood to support local business discovery and neighborhood-level SEO. Our current neighborhood coverage includes Lawrenceville, Strip District, South Side, Shadyside, East Liberty, Downtown, North Side, Oakland, Squirrel Hill, Bloomfield, Mt. Washington, and Point Breeze, with expansion planned to additional neighborhoods.

Why Editorial Content?

Unlike traditional business directories that list basic information, the Pittsburgh Business Directory provides substantial editorial content that qualifies for inclusion in major news aggregators and search index features. This approach:

  • Gives readers meaningful insight into businesses and their founders
  • Supports local SEO through comprehensive, unique content
  • Helps businesses tell their story to a wider audience
  • Provides original reporting on Pittsburgh's business community
  • Qualifies for Google News Publisher Center inclusion
